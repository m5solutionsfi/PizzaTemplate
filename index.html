Two ovens, one door

Ateş started as an argument between two cousins: one grew up rolling pizza dough in Naples, the other grinding spice for kebab in Gaziantep. Neither would give up their fire, so we built a kitchen with both — a wood oven on the left, a charcoal grill on the right, and a menu that never asks you to choose a side.

Everything is made to order once you call: dough proofed that morning, meat marinated overnight, bread from the grill, not the freezer.
